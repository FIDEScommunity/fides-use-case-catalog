# Dutch translations entered: Around the world, five country pages (final, 13 November 2026)

**Entered locally** in the TranslatePress dictionary of the design pilot (localhost only) through `learn/nl-translations.php`.
The helper fills only empty rows, so no human-edited row was overwritten (apply: filled 91, kept 695). Staging and
production were not touched. The rows are in `learn/nl-translations.json` (export: 1339 to 1430 entries; transfer status
add 0, differs 0, stale 0).

Style: u-vorm; uitgever, vertrouwende partij, houder, walletaanbieder, authentieke bron; "EUDI Wallet" stays;
"controleren (verify)" and "ophalen (retrieve)" on first use; consistent with Duitsland, Nederland, Internationaal,
Technologie; "usecase" as one word. Official names, product names and record titles stay English. Glossary definitions
are out of scope and stay English. The JS-only strip texts were not redone.

Order of work: the English changes (fact check, source status, record caveats, card texts, Learn more links) were made
first, then the full current English segments were translated. That is why many English sentences differ from the first
proposal in `nl-translation-changes-around-world-countries.md`.

Summary: 91 rows entered. Old proposal kept unchanged: 9. Revised or English changed: 10. No matching row in the first proposal: 72 (the first proposal listed body text in a different form; the Dutch was written fresh against the final English).

Review notes on the revisions: where a "revised" row exists, the English source changed in the fact-check round (for
example "checked on 13 November 2026", the CBP test, the Swiggy and BIEN caveats, the INM sentence). The Dutch follows
the new English.
