In Canada, work on digital credentials involves public bodies at different levels. The Government of Canada describes work on a common approach to sign-in and credentials for access to federal services. Provinces also publish their own information and offer services in their jurisdictions.

Federal services

The Government of Canada’s page says the Canadian Digital Service (CDS), part of Service Canada, is building two platforms: CanadaLogin, a sign-in for federal services, and GC Issue and Verify, which will let government departments issue digital versions of credentials such as work permits and boating licences. The page describes both in the future tense, calls their use optional and says CDS will work with partner departments to test features in the coming months. These are plans.

An example from British Columbia

British Columbia describes BC Wallet as an app for storing digital credentials. One example is a credential for lawyers issued through the Law Society of British Columbia. Check the current provider information to see which credentials and services are available.

The province’s digital trust documentation lists the Law Society of British Columbia as the issuer of this Lawyer Credential, available to practising lawyers since 15 January 2025. The provincial BC Wallet page, last updated on 26 October 2026, says the province is adding BC Wallet functionality to the BC Services Card app.

From national services to cross-border trade

Digital credentials can also be used between organisations. The FIDES catalog contains a Neoflow use case describing the use of digital information in a Canada–United States energy supply chain. Its account and status are those reported in the catalog; consult the parties involved for current details.

For the related U.S. customs programme, a notice in the Federal Register of 2 June 2026 says U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) will run a test of this method for crude oil transported by pipeline from Canada and Mexico. The test was to begin on 2 July 2026 and run for one year, subject to extension, modification or early termination.
