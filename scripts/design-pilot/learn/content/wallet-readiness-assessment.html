What we look at

We start with your service, the information it needs and the organisations involved. We examine the relevant roles, requirements and open questions.

The assessment can cover

  • Your service, users and required information.
  • Roles, relevant requirements and dependencies.
  • Options, open questions and possible next steps.

The scope and form of the findings are agreed with you before the assessment starts.

How it works

  1. Discuss your question. Tell us about the service or process you want to examine. We agree the scope before work starts.
  2. Examine your starting point. We review the relevant roles, information, requirements and dependencies with your team.
  3. Discuss the findings. We discuss open questions, decisions and possible next steps. The form of the findings is agreed in advance.
