The Netherlands works on the [fides_learn_term term="European Digital Identity Wallet"]European Digital Identity Wallet[/fides_learn_term] through the Dutch EDI programme and, for public data, through a separate programme for shared source access. This page describes what those programmes say about themselves.

The Dutch EDI system

Programma EDI-Stelsel NL works on the implementation of the revised eIDAS Regulation in the Netherlands. According to its background page, the programme is building a Dutch EDI system (EDI-stelsel) that fills in the European framework, is working on an implementation act, and has a public wallet, the NL Wallet, developed on its behalf. The programme falls under the responsibility of the Ministry of the Interior and Kingdom Relations.

The page also states that using a wallet is not mandatory. This page does not describe the features or availability of any Dutch wallet; consult the programme for current information.

Access to public-source data

Public bodies that hold [fides_learn_term term="Authentic Source"]authoritative data[/fides_learn_term] (in Dutch, bronhouders) can be asked to make it available in several ways. The programme Gemeenschappelijke Bronontsluiting (GBO), run by ICTU, is developing a standardised way of doing so for the EUDI Wallet, for the [fides_learn_term term="Once-Only Technical System"]Once-Only Technical System[/fides_learn_term] (OOTS) and for sharing data with private service providers on the basis of consent. ICTU says GBO is not meant to become a new, separate system but is to be anchored in existing national agreement frameworks.

The GBO design is still developing. The Globaal Ontwerp (global design), version 0.9.5 of September 2026, states that it is not yet final. ICTU’s planning lists a development phase from July to December 2026 and a realisation phase from January to December 2027. These are plans.

For the three routes and the status of the draft design, see the Dutch example in The EUDI Wallet and national implementation.

Where the information comes from

Responsible sources: Programma EDI-Stelsel NL (Ministry of the Interior and Kingdom Relations) for the EDI pages, and ICTU for GBO. Both were checked on 13 November 2026.
