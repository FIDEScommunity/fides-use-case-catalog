Why can you book a car online but still need to show your licence at pickup?

You book a hire car online. When you arrive, the rental company asks to see your original driving licence before handing over the keys. It needs to check that the licence belongs to you, is still valid and covers the vehicle. If the company checks the original at pickup, it can only complete that check once you arrive.

If the rental company accepts a mobile driving licence, it can ask for the details it needs before you travel. You review the request in your wallet before sharing. The company can verify who issued those details and whether they’ve been altered, then apply its rental rules.

Showing your licence at pickup

What you provide
You show your original driving licence to a member of staff.

What the rental company checks
Staff inspect the card and compare the photo and licence details with the driver and the booking.

What the company sees
Staff can see all the details printed on the card.

Sharing licence details before pickup

What you provide
You share the requested details from a mobile driving licence, where supported.

What the rental company checks
The service can verify the issuer and check whether the details have changed. The company still needs to check that the licence applies to the driver and vehicle and is valid for the rental.

What the company sees
If the wallet, credential and service support sharing individual details, the company can ask for just what it needs.

A business applying to become a supplier may have to find and send a permit it has already shared with another customer. Each customer then has to check it. An edited or AI-generated document can look convincing, which makes it more important to know who provided the information and whether it has changed.

Sometimes a service needs even less. An online shop checking an age limit could ask for confirmation that you’re 18 or over without asking for your date of birth. Before sharing, you should be able to see who is asking and why.
