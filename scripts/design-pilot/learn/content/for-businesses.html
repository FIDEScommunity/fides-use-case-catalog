What does your team need to check?

A supplier applies to join your procurement portal. Your team needs to identify the company and check whether the person submitting the application can act on its behalf. Start with that decision, then work out what information to exchange and how to check it.

In an online service, the question may be about the customer. For example, a cinema can ask a customer to confirm that they meet a film’s age requirement by sharing proof from an EUDI Wallet.
