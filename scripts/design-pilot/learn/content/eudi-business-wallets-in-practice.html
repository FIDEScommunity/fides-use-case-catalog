The training explains how digital wallets and verifiable credentials work and which European developments shape their adoption. Examples and demonstrations show where they apply, from digital services and onboarding to employment and business collaboration.

You try a wallet interaction yourself, using a practical example.

What you will learn

  • The basics of digital identity, wallets and verifiable credentials.
  • How the EUDI Wallet and the European Business Wallet differ and relate.
  • The roles of issuers, wallet holders and relying parties.
  • How credentials are issued, shared and verified.
  • Real use cases from across the FIDES ecosystem.
  • Opportunities and questions for your organisation, and possible next steps.

Who it is for

Policymakers, product owners, consultants, innovation managers and other professionals who want to prepare for digital wallets and verifiable credentials. No technical background is required.

What you take away

A clear picture of the wallet landscape, hands-on experience with a wallet and a first view of how wallets could support your organisation.
