This practical one-day training gives you an accessible introduction to digital wallets and credentials, the European developments shaping their adoption, and the opportunities for your organisation.

Through real-world examples, demonstrations and practical exercises, you will explore how wallets and credentials work and how they can be applied in areas such as digital services, onboarding, employment and business collaboration.

What you will learn

During the training, you will explore:

  • The fundamentals of digital identity, wallets and verifiable credentials.
  • The EUDI Wallet and European Business Wallet: developments, differences and how they relate.
  • The roles of issuers, wallet holders and relying parties.
  • How credentials are issued, shared and verified.
  • Real-world use cases from across the FIDES ecosystem.
  • Opportunities, considerations and possible next steps for your organisation.

You will also experience a wallet-based interaction yourself, using a practical example.

Who is it for?

This training is designed for policymakers, product owners, consultants, innovation managers and other professionals who want to understand and prepare for the use of digital wallets and verifiable credentials.

No technical background is required.

What will you take away?

You will leave with a clear understanding of the digital wallet landscape, practical experience with the technology and a first view of how it could support your organisation.
