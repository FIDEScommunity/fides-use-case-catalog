An interactive session for management teams and professionals in your organisation. We explain the key concepts, show how the technology works and discuss examples that fit your organisation. There is time for questions.

The session covers

  • Digital wallets and verifiable credentials: the basics.
  • Developments around the EUDI Wallet and business wallets.
  • Examples that fit your organisation.
  • Open questions and possible next steps.

For a closer look at impact and priorities, see the Wallet Readiness Workshop. For background reading, start with the knowledge topics.
