The Wallet Introduction is an interactive session for management teams and professionals who want to develop a shared understanding of digital wallets, credentials and the European digital identity landscape.

We explain the key concepts, demonstrate how the technology works and explore relevant examples for your organisation. There is ample room for questions and discussion.

The session covers

  • Digital wallets and verifiable credentials: the essentials.
  • EUDI Wallet and business wallet developments.
  • Practical examples relevant to your organisation.
  • Opportunities, questions and potential next steps.
