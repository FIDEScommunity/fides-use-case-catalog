Together, we explore relevant developments and use cases, identify the potential impact on your organisation and discuss the questions that need to be addressed. We conclude by defining initial priorities and possible next steps.

The workshop covers

  • Relevant developments in digital wallets and credentials.
  • Your organisation’s potential roles as an issuer, relying party or wallet user.
  • Opportunities in existing services, processes and partnerships.
  • Key considerations for trust, privacy, governance and implementation.
  • An initial view of priorities and next steps.

The workshop is tailored to your organisation and can focus on the EUDI Wallet, business wallets or their combination.
