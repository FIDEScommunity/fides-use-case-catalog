Your team works through relevant developments and use cases, looks at the possible impact on your organisation and lists the questions to settle. The workshop ends with first priorities and possible next steps. It suits business, policy and technical stakeholders together.

The workshop covers

  • Developments in digital wallets and credentials that matter to you.
  • Possible roles for your organisation: issuer, relying party or wallet user.
  • Use cases in your services, processes and partnerships.
  • Points to settle on trust, privacy, governance and implementation.
  • First priorities and possible next steps.

We tailor the workshop to your organisation. It can focus on the EUDI Wallet, on business wallets, or on both. See use cases from the FIDES ecosystem for examples.
