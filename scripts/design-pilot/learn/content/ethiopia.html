Ethiopia’s National ID Program describes Fayda as a foundational digital identity programme for residents. It issues a Fayda Identification Number after registration and provides ways to verify identity in services.

Credentials and verification

The programme describes several forms of Fayda identification, including a digital copy and a physical card. The programme lists a downloadable paper form (eFayda), a digital copy in the National ID mini app inside the telebirr super app, and a physical ID card on request.

Its authentication information describes online and offline verification methods. Offline verification uses a QR code on the Fayda credential. Online verification includes eKYC with a one-time password, a selfie or a fingerprint scan. The suitability of a method depends on the service and the arrangements in place.

FaydaPass and its status

The programme’s authentication page, consulted on 13 November 2026, says it is working on FaydaPass, a digital credential wallet intended to serve as a credential repository for citizens and for multiple use cases, not only Fayda credentials. That page does not describe the wallet as available. An app named FaydaPass, published by the National ID Program, is listed in Google Play (checked on 13 November 2026), so the programme’s published information does not describe the wallet’s status consistently. This page therefore does not state which credentials the wallet issues or which institutions use it. Check the programme for current status.

The FIDES catalog includes a FaydaPass use case submitted by the National ID Program. Read the record alongside the programme’s current information to understand the intended interaction and its development status.
