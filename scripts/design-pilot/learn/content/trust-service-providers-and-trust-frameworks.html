What is a trust service?

Examples include services for electronic signatures, electronic seals and timestamps. A service can help establish who signed or sealed information, or when a particular action took place. What a check establishes depends on the service and the context in which it is used.

An organisation may use trust services alongside wallet or credential software. A trust service provider can also perform another role in an interaction, depending on the service it offers.

What does qualified mean?

Under the EU eIDAS framework, some trust services and their providers have qualified status. The qualification applies to specific services, so it is important to check both the provider and the service. National trusted lists record qualified providers and their qualified services.

A FIDES catalog entry can help you find a provider. For an authoritative check of qualified status, consult the applicable national trusted list through the European Commission’s trusted-list resources. Check the relevant service and its status at the relevant time.

What is a trust framework?

A trust framework describes agreements and rules that participants use in an ecosystem. It may address who can participate, how parties identify each other, which technical specifications they use and how responsibilities are divided.

A trust framework and a trust service are related concepts. A framework may refer to services that participants use, while a trust service performs a particular function within an interaction.

Finding providers

The FIDES organisation catalog includes an eIDAS trust service provider role and a QTSP certification filter. Use the filters to explore relevant organisations and, where available, information about their services. Check the underlying source and current status before relying on a qualification.
