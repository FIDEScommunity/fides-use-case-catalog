Digital wallets differ in what they support and who provides them. Before choosing one, consider what you want to use it for and whether the organisations you deal with support it.

Questions to ask

  • Who provides the wallet, and where can you find support?
  • Does it work on your device and support the information you want to receive?
  • What does it show you before you share information?
  • What options are available if you lose your device or change phones?
  • What information does the provider collect, and where can you read its privacy information?

The answers depend on the product. Consult the provider’s documentation before relying on a recovery or backup option.

If your device is lost or replaced

Follow the instructions of your wallet provider. Depending on the wallet and credential, you may also need to contact an issuer to block access, recover information or obtain it again. Keep any recovery information according to the provider’s instructions.

Compare available wallets

The personal wallet catalog lists wallets and information about their features. Use it to find options, then check current details with the provider. A catalog listing does not by itself establish that a wallet is suitable for your situation or accepted by a particular service.
