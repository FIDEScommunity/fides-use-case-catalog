Digital wallets differ in what they support and who provides them. Before choosing one, consider what you want to use it for and whether the organisations you deal with support it.

Which wallets can you use?

Start with the service or sector you want to use. A service may tell you which wallets it accepts. A sector initiative may specify which wallets work within its trust framework.

For an EUDI Wallet, check the options published by your country. A Member State can provide a wallet itself, mandate a provider or recognise a solution. If you want to use the wallet to prove your official identity, check how the designated provider issues Person Identification Data (PID) to the wallet you choose. You need valid PID in the wallet to prove your official identity using PID.

Questions to ask

  • Who provides the wallet, and where can you find support?
  • Does it work on your device and support the information you want to receive?
  • What does it show you before you share information?
  • What options are available if you lose your device or change phones?
  • What information does the provider collect, and where can you read its privacy information?

The answers depend on the product. Consult the provider’s documentation before relying on a recovery or backup option.

If your device is lost or replaced

Some credentials are tied to a security key on your phone. If that key cannot be transferred, the issuer may need to issue the credential again for your new phone. An ordinary phone backup may restore the app without restoring access to those credentials.

If you still have your old phone, follow the wallet provider’s transfer instructions before resetting it. If your phone is lost, check how to block or revoke access to the old wallet and how to get the credentials you need again.

Compare available wallets

The personal wallet catalog lists wallets and information about their features. Use it to find options, then check current details with the provider. A catalog listing does not by itself establish that a wallet is suitable for your situation or accepted by a particular service.
