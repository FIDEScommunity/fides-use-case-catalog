A school, public authority or other organisation may offer information that you can receive in a digital wallet. It might be a qualification, a permit or another statement about you. The organisation providing it is called the [fides_learn_term term="Issuer"]issuer[/fides_learn_term].

Before you add something

Check which organisation is issuing the information and whether you recognise the request. Look at what the information says, who it is about and whether it has an expiry date. The issuer or your wallet provider can explain what to do if something looks wrong.

What happens if information changes?

Some information stays relevant for a long time. Other information may change or expire. Depending on the credential, an issuer may update, replace or withdraw it. Check with the issuer if you need a correction or a new credential.
