Making a request

A request should be connected to a specific service or process. A service checking eligibility for a role, for example, may need to know whether a person has a qualification. It should consider which information is necessary for that decision.

The wallet user can respond to the request using information available in their wallet. The interaction depends on the wallet, the credential and the service involved.

Checking the information

The receiving service may need to check who issued the credential, whether the proof is valid and whether status information is available. The checks depend on the credential and the rules applicable to the process.

A technical check helps establish whether the credential and its proof meet particular requirements. The organisation still has to decide whether the information is sufficient for its purpose. A valid qualification, for instance, does not automatically establish that it meets every requirement for a job.

Relying party and verifier

[fides_learn_term term="Verifier"]A verifier[/fides_learn_term] performs technical checks. A relying party uses the information in its process. Depending on the arrangement, the same organisation can perform both roles or use a provider for verification.
