Honduras has introduced BIEN, the Billetera Electrónica Nacional. DIGER, the government body responsible for the programme, describes BIEN as an official mobile application through which users can obtain a digital identity credential.

Public credentials in one wallet

A wallet can bring together information from different public bodies. Which credentials are available, who can receive them and where they are accepted depend on the service and the current national arrangements. Consult DIGER for instructions and current availability.

DIGER says BIEN is available for Android and iOS. It lists the biometric national identity document (from the National Registry of Persons, RNP), the driving licence (from the transport directorate, DNVT), the firearms carry permit (from the National Police) and the vehicle inspection slip (from the Property Institute). Its FAQ says the residence card for foreign residents, from the National Immigration Institute (INM), will be added later, while its overview page lists that card among the supported credentials.

See it in practice

A FIDES catalog use case describes several Honduran public authorities issuing credentials into BIEN. The catalog attributes the description and status to the submitting organisation. Its list of issuing authorities differs in places from DIGER’s own pages, so check the record and official programme information for current details.
