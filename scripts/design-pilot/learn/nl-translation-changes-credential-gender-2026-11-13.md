# "Het credential": gender correction, 13 November 2026 (local pilot only)

Editorial decision: in Dutch running text "credential" is a het-word: "het credential", "dat credential", "hetzelfde credential", "een digitaal
credential", "welk credential". The export (`learn/nl-translations.json`) and the local database held nine translations with a literal "de credential"
and four more rows with a wrong article, adjective or pronoun for the same noun (13 rows in total). They were rewritten locally with
`wp eval-file learn/nl-translations.php correct` (list `$corrections`, block "Round 2026-11-13 (credential gender)"); a row is only rewritten while it
still holds the expected old Dutch, so no translation entered by a person is overwritten. Afterwards `learn/nl-translations-transfer.php export`
and `status` (add 0, differs 0). Nothing was run on staging or production.

Scope: all 1454 exported rows were searched for "de/die/deze/dezelfde/welke/elke credential", an adjective in -e before "credential" ("een digitale ...")
and the pronouns in the surrounding sentences ("die", "deze", "hij", "zij"). Rows that already read correctly were left alone ("Een soort credential",
"geen afzonderlijk credential", "Heeft ieder credential dezelfde status?" where "dezelfde" belongs to the de-word "status", "een digitale credential-wallet"
where the compound ends in the de-word "wallet", "Lawyer Credential" as a product name).

"Ontvangende partij" in the Technology article: the English source says "receiving party" / "receiving service" in the generic sense (the party that gets
the information), not the role "relying party". Per `learn/nl-terminology.md` ("A party that receives information is not in every context a relying party")
"ontvangende partij" / "ontvangende dienst" stay; the Technology pages contain no "relying party" in English, so no "vertrouwende partij" was needed there.

## Check

`tests/nl-terminology-checks.cjs` reads the main text of all 57 manifest pages at 1440 and 390 px and fails on "de/dezelfde/welke/deze/die/iedere/elke
credential" and on "een/geen ...e credential" (de-word adjective). Result after this round: no hits at both widths.
