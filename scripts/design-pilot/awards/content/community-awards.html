About the awards

The FIDES Community Awards recognize practical applications of business wallets and verifiable credentials. They give visibility to solutions that others can learn from, adopt and build upon.

The 2026 edition featured three categories: Impact, Innovation and Best Business Wallet. Results were determined through a combination of 50% expert jury evaluation and 50% community voting, with 500+ votes cast.

The FIDES Community Awards and the Global Digital Trust Awards ran alongside each other, recognizing different contributions to digital trust. They brought together 94 published use cases from 29 countries, involving 210+ organizations, with 15 finalists selected across three award categories. An entry could only be a finalist in one category across the two competitions.

The submitted solutions remain discoverable in the FIDES Trust Explorer. Organizations can continue to enrich their entries with videos, images, links and implementation experience, helping others find relevant solutions and understand how they work.

A compelling example of how a trusted, reusable and revocable digital credential can improve both efficiency and social impact. By transforming the Dutch Certificate of Good Conduct into a verifiable credential, the solution can simplify employment and contracting processes while helping organizations protect vulnerable people.

Rank 2

Making the European Business Wallet real: EBW trial for national healthcare data exchange (KIK-V)

A powerful demonstration of how the emerging European Business Wallet can strengthen trust in an existing national healthcare data exchange. By combining organizational identity, mandates, qualified electronic signatures and trusted data exchange, the trial shows how new European trust infrastructure can be integrated into highly regulated, real-world business processes.

A highly scalable approach to privacy-preserving age verification, built on an open-source solution and enabling online services to accept wallet-based proof of age without having to build and maintain their own wallet infrastructure. An ambitious application of Digital Product Passports and verifiable credentials, rooted in deep experience of the textile industry and its complex supply chains. Developed in the context of the European CIRPASS-2 initiative and aligned with UNTP, this approach uses independently verifiable data to support compliance, transparency and more responsible production.

A highly relevant approach to making AI-driven decisions more transparent and accountable, particularly when they affect people’s access to jobs, housing, credit or other important opportunities. Innovation Award

Rank 1 · Winner 2026

Preventing SIM Swap Fraud with EUDI Wallets and AI Voice Agents

An original combination of EUDI Wallets and AI voice agents to provide strong identity verification during phone interactions. The same pattern can also be used with human operators, addressing a broader challenge of establishing trust during phone calls. It could extend well beyond telecom to sectors such as banking and other trusted remote interactions.

View use case →

Rank 2

Smart Government Applications Powered by Digital Wallets

A practical combination of digital wallets and AI to simplify complex government processes, demonstrated through a public procurement use case. It shows how information that today moves through cumbersome and often difficult-to-verify paper and PDF flows — such as certificates, registry extracts and mandates — can instead be provided as trusted, machine-readable credentials, with Business Wallets providing the tooling to manage and exchange them while AI helps businesses navigate and complete the process.

View use case →

Rank 3

Spherity’s Digital Product Passports for BVG’s Electric Bus Batteries

A strong real-world application of Digital Product Passports to electric bus batteries already operating in public transport. By making trusted product and lifecycle information digitally accessible, the project demonstrates how emerging DPP infrastructure can support regulatory compliance and more transparent, circular industrial supply chains.

Rank 4

Verified machine safety access at Koning Willem I College (KW1C)

A tangible example of how digital wallets and credentials can also be used to control access in the physical world. Using a wallet and NFC, access to machinery can be linked directly to verified qualifications or safety credentials. The approach provides a strong demonstrator for similar applications in education, industry and workplace environments.

A compelling example of how Personal and Business Wallets can work together to simplify company formation and interaction with government. By combining trusted attestations with automation, the approach aims to reduce administrative burden for legitimate businesses while strengthening safeguards against fraud and misuse.

Award categories and evaluation criteria

The first edition of the FIDES Community Awards recognized achievements in three categories.

Best Business Wallet 2026

Recognizing business wallet solutions that help organizations put verifiable credentials into practice.

Evaluation criteria included:

  • Real-world adoption
  • Business wallet features
  • User experience
  • Sustainable business model

Impact Award 2026

Recognizing use cases that demonstrate the value of verifiable credentials in practice.

Evaluation criteria included:

  • Real-world adoption
  • Economic value
  • Societal impact
  • Privacy
  • Scalability
  • Regulatory readiness and compliance

Innovation Award 2026

Recognizing new approaches to applying digital trust technologies and enabling new services.

Evaluation criteria included:

  • Originality of the approach
  • Novel use of technology
  • Ecosystem innovation
  • Potential to enable new use cases or services
  • Innovative business model
  • Innovative governance model
Expert jury

The FIDES Community Awards 2026 were supported by an independent expert jury with experience in digital identity, business wallets and trusted digital ecosystems:

  • Rob Brand — Ministry of Economic Affairs / WE BUILD
  • Annet Steenbergen — Independent Digital Identity Expert
  • Joran Frik — Senior Manager Digital Identity, Deloitte Cyber
  • Jacob Boersma — Independent Digital Identity Expert

The jury evaluated the finalists against the criteria for each award category. Its evaluation contributed 50% of the final result, with community voting accounting for the remaining 50%.

We thank the jury members for their time, expertise and careful consideration of the entries.
