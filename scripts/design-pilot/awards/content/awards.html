About the awards

The awards recognise real-world applications of verifiable credentials and digital wallets, and bring people together to share practical experience.

The first edition was announced in Geneva in 2026 during Global Digital Collaboration. The 2026 edition brought together 94 published use cases from 29 countries, involving 210+ organizations, with 24 finalists selected across five award categories.

The finalists were evaluated through a 50/50 combination of an international jury representing digital trust associations from around the world and community voting, with 3,000+ votes cast.

All award submissions remain available in the FIDES use case catalog and can continue to be updated and enriched.

Award categories and voting guidelines

The first edition of the Global Digital Trust Awards awarded five categories.

Collaboration Award 2026

Award for the use case that best demonstrates collaboration across organizations, sectors, regions, or ecosystems.

Guidelines for voting include:

  • Multi-stakeholder collaboration
  • Cross-border or cross-ecosystem interoperability
  • Ecosystem growth and participation
  • Open standards and reusability
  • Knowledge sharing and community engagement
  • Contribution to the broader digital trust ecosystem

Social Impact Award 2026

Award for the use case that demonstrates the greatest positive societal impact through the use of digital wallets, verifiable credentials, and trusted digital infrastructure.

Guidelines for voting include:

  • Societal impact
  • Inclusion and accessibility
  • Privacy and user empowerment
  • Contribution to sustainability or public good
  • Measurable benefits for citizens, communities, or organizations
  • Potential for global adoption and replication

Adoption Award 2026

Award for the use case that demonstrates the strongest real-world adoption and operational use.

Guidelines for voting include:

  • Real-world deployment and usage
  • Number and diversity of participants
  • Ecosystem adoption and engagement
  • Scalability and sustainability
  • Business or operational value
  • Regulatory readiness and compliance

Best Wallet Award 2026

Award for the wallet solution that best demonstrates excellence in usability, interoperability, trust, and ecosystem support.

Guidelines for voting include:

  • User experience and accessibility
  • Interoperability and standards support
  • Security and privacy
  • Ecosystem integration
  • Real-world adoption
  • Innovation and future readiness

Business Value Award 2026

Award for the use case that best demonstrates measurable business value using digital wallets and verifiable credentials..

Guidelines for voting include:

  • Friction removal and efficiency
  • Revenue growth
  • Better experience for customers, partners, employees
  • Risk reduction and increased trust
  • Scalability and long-term business sustainability
  • Systematic fraud response
2026 international jury

The 2026 results combined international jury assessment and community voting, each contributing 50%. The jury represented digital trust associations from around the world; more than 3,000 votes were cast in the community vote.
